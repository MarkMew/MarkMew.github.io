My interest in web development and cloud technology began with web courses at university.

At first, I tended to follow the standard approaches in textbooks. After I started working, I gradually realized that real-world problems rarely have one standard answer. That led me to revisit what I had learned and think about how to apply it within real systems, teams, and constraints.

I have worked with Java and Python. After moving through different jobs and environments, I came to realize that what I enjoy most is breaking down problems, understanding their context, and growing through the process of solving them.

Working with different kinds of requirements and teams has also taught me to organize, explain, and share what I learn. Rather than simply listing technical terms, I try to put what I know into language that is familiar to the people I am talking with. That helps people from different backgrounds understand the problem, join the discussion, and find a workable solution together.

大學時期修過 Web 相關課程後，我開始對網站開發與雲端技術產生興趣。

一開始，我習慣追隨教科書裡的標準做法；直到進入職場後才慢慢發現，實務現場往往沒有唯一的標準答案。於是我開始重新整理過去學到的知識，思考如何把它們落地到真實的系統、團隊與限制之中。

我寫過 Java，也寫過 Python，輾轉經歷不同工作與場域後，逐漸發現自己真正喜歡的，是拆解問題、釐清脈絡，並在解決問題的過程中感受到自己持續成長。

因為接觸過不同類型的需求與團隊，我也養成了整理、表達與分享的習慣。比起單純堆疊技術名詞，我更擅長把已知的技術知識轉換成對方熟悉的語言，讓不同背景的人也能理解問題、參與討論，並一起找到可行的解法。
